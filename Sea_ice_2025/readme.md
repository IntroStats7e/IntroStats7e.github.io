# Sea ice 2025

Climate scientists have been observing the extent of sea ice using satellite observations. Many have expressed concern because, since 1980, the extent of sea ice has declined precipitously—possibly due to global climate change. But a multiple regression of Arctic_MaxExtent on Mean_global_temperature and Year gives a coefficient for temp that is essentially zero.

The data are in `Sea_ice_2025.txt`


This dataset is sourced from: Ice extent from http://nsidc.org; temperature from https://data.giss.nasa.gov/gistemp/
