# NLSCYA child sex order

The National Longitudinal Surveys are nationally representative surveys sponsored by the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. This data extract includes information on the sex and year of birth for first and second children for participants whose first child was male. 

The data are in `NLSCYA_child_sex_order.txt`
