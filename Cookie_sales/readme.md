# Cookie sales

A company that sells cookies decided to redesign their packaging with brighter colors with the hope of improving sales. They chose a random sample of 50 stores to pilot the new packaging, which holds the same amount of product at the same price point as before. Over the period of a week, the 50 stores recorded sales of the brand of cookies in question.

The data are in `Cookie_sales.txt`
