# Hurricane frequencies 2024

The National Hurricane Center lists the numbers of large (category 3, 4, or 5) hurricanes that have struck the United States by decade since 1851. The data are given here.

The data are in `Hurricane_frequencies_2024.txt`


This dataset is sourced from: http://www.aoml.noaa.gov/hrd/hurdat/All_U.S._Hurricanes.html
