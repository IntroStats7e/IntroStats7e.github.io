# ATUS 2024 sample 100

The American Time Use Survey (ATUS) is an annual survey of Americans over the age of 15 conducted by the Bureau of Labor Statistics (http://www.bls.gov) to better understand how Americans spend their time over the course of a 24 hour period. This is a random sample of 100 (seed = 1) from R. 

The data are in `ATUS_2024_sample_100.txt`
