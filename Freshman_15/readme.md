# Freshman 15

Is it true that students tend to gain weight during their first year in college? Cornell Professor of Nutrition David Levitsky recruited students from two large sections of an introductory health course. Although they were volunteers, they appeared to match the rest of the freshman class in terms of demographic variables such as sex and ethnicity. The students were weighed during the first week of the semester, then again 12 weeks later. Based on Professor Levitsky’s data, estimate the mean weight gain in first-semester freshmen and comment on the “freshman 15.” (Weights are in pounds.)

The data are in `Freshman_15.txt`


This dataset is sourced from: Prof David Levitsky, Cornell University
