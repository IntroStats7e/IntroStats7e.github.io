# Full time work 2024

The American Time Use Survey (ATUS) is an annual survey of Americans over the age of 15 conducted by the Bureau of Labor Statistics (http://www.bls.gov) to better understand how Americans spend their time over the course of a 24 hour period. The most recent data available as of January 2026 is the 2024 survey data. 

The data are in `Full_time_work_2024.txt`
