# Chips Ahoy!

In 1998, as an advertising campaign, the Nabisco Company announced a “1000 Chips Challenge,” claiming that every 18-ounce bag of their Chips Ahoy! cookies contained at least 1000 chocolate chips. Dedicated statistics students at the Air Force Academy randomly selected bags of cookies and counted the chocolate chips. The data report their counts.

The data are in `Chips_Ahoy!.txt`
