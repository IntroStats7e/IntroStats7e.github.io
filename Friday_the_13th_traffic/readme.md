# Friday the 13th traffic

Many people fear Friday the 13th as an unlucky day. Researchers looked into this to see whether there were differences in traffic or in admissions to hospitals for road accidents on Friday 13th when compared with the adjacent Friday 6th.

The data are in `Friday_the_13th_traffic.txt`


This dataset is sourced from: British Medical Journal Is Friday the 13th bad for your health? BMJ 1993;307:1584
