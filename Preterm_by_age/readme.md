# Preterm by age

Most pregnancies are full-term, but some are preterm (less than 37 weeks). Of those that are preterm. the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention classifies them as early (less than 34 weeks) and late (34 to 36 weeks). We will use National Vital Statistics Report to examine those outcomes in the United States broken down by age of the mother.

The data are in `Preterm_by_age.txt`


This dataset is sourced from: NVSS data from 2024 (in All_babies_2024).
