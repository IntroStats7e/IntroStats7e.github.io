# Education by age 2024

The data, taken from a U.S. Census report published in September 2025 (https://www.census.gov/data/tables/2024/demo/educational-attainment/cps-detailed-tables.html), show education level vs age for a number of United States adults.

The data are in `Education_by_age_2024.txt`
