# Exam time

The instructor of a large math class estimates that at the intended level of difficulty, his undergraduate midterm exams require on average 10 minutes for graduate students in his department to complete. If the average is longer than 10 minutes, then the exam is likely too hard and needs to be revised. As usual, the professor gathers his 20 teaching assistants in a room to have them test out the exam. The  dataset holds the times it took the  grad students to complete the exam.

The data are in `Exam_time.txt`
